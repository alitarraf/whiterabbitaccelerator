What We Learned
BUILT ON
THE BEST.

We studied YC, Techstars, and 500 Global — then adapted what works for small-town talent and community-first entrepreneurship.

Y Combinator

Talk to Users. Build. Iterate.

Weekly milestones, deep customer discovery, and a high-stakes Demo Day create undeniable accountability. Peer cohorts generate organic pressure and knowledge that no solo founder gets alone.

Techstars

Mentorship at the Center.

The "Give First" philosophy puts expert mentors beside founders from day one — not in a lecture hall, but in real 1:1s. 74% of Techstars companies raise capital within 3 years because relationships open doors.

500 Global

Great Ideas Exist Everywhere.

500 Global proved world-class support doesn't require a tech hub. Their 4-month format and diverse cohorts produce more resilient businesses — and that blueprint works in Ashland just as well as Austin.

White Rabbit Accelerator
Our Edge

Your Town Is Your Unfair Advantage.

Big-city accelerators ship you back home after Demo Day. We start there. Your customers, suppliers, and supporters are already in the room — and so are we, for 14 months after you join.

No equity sold. No debt taken. No relocation required. Just real mentorship, real resources, and a community that shows up.

The Rogue Valley's economic development depends on measurable results. Every job created, every dollar of new revenue, every partnership formed by a White Rabbit graduate is documented and reported. This data makes the accelerator fundable — so more founders get the support you received.
